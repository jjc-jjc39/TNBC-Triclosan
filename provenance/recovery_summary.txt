------------------------

列：gene, source
gene字段唯一值：251

重要说明：
1. 这张表是项目当时实际使用/保存的原始候选表，本恢复包中的

   “multi-database+pug”。因此旧稿中“Supplementary Table S1提供每个基因
   的source database和evidence type”这一说法与现存原表不一致。

五、建议用于当前论文的严谨表述
------------------------------
建议不要写：
“A total of 251 unique human triclosan target genes were identified...”

建议写成类似：
“An initial triclosan-related candidate resource contained 251 deduplicated
entries assembled from literature/database curation and PubChem bioassay-derived
records. Because the archived raw candidate file also contained assay-related
strings that did not map to the TCGA gene-symbol space, downstream panel
derivation was based on entries mapping to the analyzed TCGA gene identifiers.
Sixty-six archived candidate entries mapped to the TCGA analyzed gene space,
of which 24 met the prespecified differential-expression criteria; these 24
genes were locked before external validation.”

这段表述能够被本恢复包中的原始文件和核验结果直接支持。

六、分析冻结原则
----------------
本次工作属于“历史来源与可重复性恢复/审计”，没有重新筛选基因、改变阈值、
改变随机种子或新增结果导向分析。原24基因和后续冻结分析均未改变。
